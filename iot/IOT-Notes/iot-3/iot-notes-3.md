## Actuators: Definition and Function

- An actuator is a component of a machine or system that moves or controls the mechanism or the system.
- It is the mechanism by which a control system acts upon an environment.
- An actuator requires both a control signal and a source of energy.

### How Actuators Work

- Upon receiving a control signal, the actuator converts energy into mechanical motion.
- The control system can be simple (e.g., a fixed mechanical or electronic system), software-based (e.g., a printer driver, robot control system), a human, or any other input.

---

## Types of Actuators

### 1. Hydraulic Actuators

- Consist of a cylinder or fluid motor that uses hydraulic power to facilitate mechanical operation.
- Mechanical motion is converted to linear, rotary, or oscillatory motion.
- Exert considerable force because liquids are nearly impossible to compress.
- Their usage is restricted by limited acceleration.

### 2. Pneumatic Actuators

- Convert energy from a vacuum or compressed air at high pressure into either linear or rotary motion.
- Examples: pneumatic rack and pinion actuators are used for valve controls of water pipes.
- Respond quickly to starting and stopping signals.
- Enable large forces to be produced from relatively small pressure changes (e.g., pneumatic brakes are very responsive to small changes in pressure applied by the driver).
- Responsible for converting pressure into force.

### 3. Electric Actuators

- Generally powered by a motor that converts electrical energy into mechanical torque.
- Electrical energy is used to actuate equipment such as solenoid valves, which control the flow of water in pipes in response to electrical signals.
- Considered among the cheapest, cleanest, and speediest actuator types available.

**Examples**

- A solenoid-based electric bell ringing mechanism.
- A motor-drive-based rotary actuator.

### 4. Thermal or Magnetic Actuators

- Can be actuated by applying thermal or magnetic energy.
- Tend to be compact, lightweight, economical, and have high power density.
- Popular manufacturers: *Finnish Modti Inc.* and *American Dynalloy*.

**Example**

- A coilgun works on the principle of magnetic actuation.

### 5. Mechanical Actuators

- Convert rotary motion into linear motion to execute movement.
- Involve gears, rails, pulleys, chains, and other devices to operate.
- Example: rack and pinion mechanism.

### 6. Soft Actuators

- (e.g., polymer-based) are designed to handle fragile objects, such as in fruit harvesting in agriculture or manipulating internal organs in biomedicine.
- Typically address challenging tasks in robotics.
- Produce flexible motion due to the integration of microscopic changes at the molecular level into a macroscopic deformation of the actuator materials.

### 7. Shape Memory Polymers (SMP) Actuators

- Function similarly to muscles, responding to a range of stimuli such as light, electrical, magnetic, heat, pH, and moisture changes.
- Exhibit surprising features such as low density, high strain recovery, biocompatibility, and biodegradability.
