Scenario:
In a large smart hospital, hundreds of connected devices—such as patient monitors, infusion pumps, wearable sensors, and diagnostic machines—are constantly being added, removed, or moved between departments. For the system to function safely and efficiently, every device must be recognized, understood, and coordinated with others in real time.

Question:
In a smart hospital environment where medical devices are continuously being introduced or relocated, how does the system automatically detect and integrate these devices into its network? What technologies enable this process? Additionally, explain why it is essential for each device to share its identity, functional capabilities, and current status to ensure reliable communication and coordination across the system.
