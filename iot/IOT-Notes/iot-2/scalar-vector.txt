# Scalar and Vector Sensor

**Scalar Sensor:**
Scalar Sensors are those types of sensors that 
produces signals proportional to the quantity 
being measured

Example: Temperature, Weight

**Vector Sensor:**
Vector Sensor are those types of sensors that
produces signals proportional to the quantity 
and direction

Example: Compass, Acceleration, velocity

**Hysteresis Error**
Error that caused by the sensors output value
