## **Scenario: Smart Campus Network Deployment**

A private university is deploying an IoT-based smart campus system. The system includes:

* Smart attendance devices

* CCTV cameras

* Smart classroom controllers

* Environmental monitoring sensors

The IT department is using **Raspberry Pi devices** in different parts of the campus. Each Raspberry Pi can operate in different WiFi modes:

* **Client Mode** (connects to an existing WiFi network)

* **Soft AP Mode** (acts as a WiFi hotspot for other devices)

* **Monitor Mode** (captures and analyzes WiFi traffic for security monitoring)

The university management wants to understand how these modes affect operations, security, and cost before approving the final architecture.

## **Question**

As a technology consultant:

1. Explain the business purpose of using Raspberry Pi devices in Client Mode, Soft AP Mode, and Monitor Mode within this smart campus system.

2. Recommend where each mode should be used and justify your reasoning.

3. Identify potential security risks associated with these WiFi modes.

4. Discuss how incorrect configuration could impact university operations and reputation.

You may draw a simple diagram to support your explanation.
