# AURA (Adaptive Universal Robotic Assistant)

AURA is a mobile, AI-powered IoT guardian designed to combine home automation, emotional interaction, and active safety in a single system.

---

## Core Innovation

A predictive, autonomous, and emotionally aware system that observes, understands, and acts proactively to ensure safety and comfort.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Emotional Interaction:** Face and voice analysis for mood and presence detection
- **Smart Home Control:** Similar to Alexa/Siri but mobile (supports appliance control)
- **Safety Detection:** Monitors for fire, smoke, gas, and water leakage
- **Elderly Care:** Fall detection and continuous health monitoring
- **Child Care:** Detects crying and distress
- **Autonomous Patrol:** Live indoor patrol with video streaming
- **Environmental Monitoring:** Measures CO₂, temperature, humidity, and more

---

## System Architecture

- **Hardware:** Raspberry Pi / ESP32, sensors, camera, motors, GSM module
- **Network:** WiFi & GSM connectivity; supports MQTT & HTTP protocols
- **Cloud:** AI processing, data storage, and alert handling
- **Application:** Mobile app dashboard for remote control and monitoring

---

## Functional Requirements

- Detect hazards and monitor human conditions
- Send alerts via SMS, phone call, or mobile app
- Remotely control home appliances
- Navigate and patrol indoor environments autonomously

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

- Low latency
- High reliability
- Strong security
- Energy efficiency
- Scalability

---

## Vision

AURA aspires to be a living, protective presence within your home—constantly attentive, adaptive, and supportive of all its inhabitants.
